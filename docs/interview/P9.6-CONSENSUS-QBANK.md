# P9.6 — Consensus / Split-Brain (CTO Q-bank)

**Q1. Core problem?** Partition → competing promotions → dual writers.  
**Q2. Safety invariant?** ≤1 authoritative financial writer.  
**Q3. Algorithm?** Raft-style roles + quorum votes + durable term/vote.  
**Q4. Quorum formula?** floor(N/2)+1.  
**Q5. 3-node minority?** 1 node cannot elect.  
**Q6. Roles?** Follower, Candidate, Leader.  
**Q7. Term?** Monotonic; higher supersedes; never decrease.  
**Q8. Vote rule?** One vote per term; durable votedFor.  
**Q9. Self-vote alone multi-node?** Insufficient without quorum.  
**Q10. Financial WAL vs consensus?** Separate; no Event.Sequence on votes.  
**Q11. Authority gate?** consensus.Node.AuthorizeCommand = IsLeader.  
**Q12. Stale leader Submit?** ErrAuthorityRejected; zero mutation.  
**Q13. Partition A|BC?** BC may elect; A cannot.  
**Q14. Total partition?** Zero leaders OK.  
**Q15. Heal?** Higher term; old steps down; one writer.  
**Q16. Restart term?** Recovered from durable meta; not 0.  
**Q17. Election safety log?** LastLogIndex = financial seq watermark.  
**Q18. Heartbeat?** AppendEntries empty; resets election timer.  
**Q19. Leases?** Not primary safety (quorum+term is).  
**Q20. Two leaders same term?** Forbidden by vote uniqueness.  
**Q21. Safety vs liveness?** Safety wins under partition.  
**Q22. Client during election?** Reject unavailable; no auto-retry.  
**Q23. P9.5 boundary?** P9.5 shared registry; P9.6 quorum.  
**Q24. P9.4 replication?** Still ships financial WAL; consensus is authority.  
**Q25. Dual-writer proof?** CountAuthoritativeWriters + service tests.  
**Q26. Metrics?** term, election, is_leader, authority_rej — bounded.  
**Q27. BFT?** Out of scope.  
**Q28. K8s?** Out of scope.  
**Q29. External store?** Not required.  
**Q30. Exactly-one-writer?** TESTED elect + partition heal + total partition.
